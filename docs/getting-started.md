# Getting started

[← All guides](../README.md)

## What you need

- Your Pixel Xpress display
- Power for the display: a USB cable and USB power adapter, or track power if your display is
  built into a powered car
- A phone with the **Pixel Xpress** app and **Bluetooth turned on**
- Your home **Wi-Fi name and password**. The display needs a **2.4 GHz** network; it can't join
  5 GHz-only networks.

## Set up your first display

1. **Power the display.** After a few seconds it shows the Pixel Xpress logo.
2. **Open the Pixel Xpress app** on your phone and allow Bluetooth and local network access when
   asked. The app uses these to find and talk to your display.
3. Tap **Scan Devices**.
4. The first time, the app asks for your Wi-Fi details (**Initial Wi-Fi Setup**). Enter the
   **Wi-Fi SSID** (network name) and **Wi-Fi Password**, then tap **Save & Continue**.
5. On **Select Device**, tap your display in the list. Displays show up with names starting
   with **pxe**. If more than one is listed, the one with the strongest signal (the number closest
   to 0 dBm) is usually the nearest.
6. Give the display a name you'll recognize, for example "Caboose", and tap **Save Device**.
7. The app sends your Wi-Fi details to the display over Bluetooth, and the display connects to
   your Wi-Fi. This takes up to a minute.
8. The display now appears on the app's main screen. A **green upload icon** next to it means
   it's online and ready.

Next: [upload your pictures](mobile-app.md#upload-pictures-or-a-slideshow).

## Add more displays

Repeat the steps above for each display. Displays you've already added don't appear in the scan
list again. All your displays use the same Wi-Fi details from the app's **Settings**.
