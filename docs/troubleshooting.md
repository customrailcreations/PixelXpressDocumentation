# Troubleshooting

[← All guides](../README.md)

## The display doesn't appear when I scan

- Make sure the display is powered and **Bluetooth is on** in your phone.
- Allow the Pixel Xpress app to use Bluetooth (and on iPhone, local network access) in your
  phone's settings.
- Displays you've already added aren't listed again; they're on the main screen.
- A display that's already on Wi-Fi can still be found. If it isn't, hold **Y for 5 seconds** on
  the display to put it back into setup mode, then scan again.
- Move the phone closer to the display.

## The display won't join my Wi-Fi

- The display needs a **2.4 GHz** Wi-Fi network. If your router has separate 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz
  names, use the 2.4 GHz one.
- Check the Wi-Fi name and password in the app's ⚙ **Settings**. They're case-sensitive.
- Make sure the display is within Wi-Fi range.
- Then hold **Y for 5 seconds** on the display, delete it in the app, and add it again.

## The app says "Device Disconnected" or shows a red Wi-Fi icon

- Check the display is powered. Press **Y** on it: the info screen shows its IP address, or
  "not connected".
- Make sure your **phone is on the same Wi-Fi** as the display (not mobile data or a guest network).
- After a power cut, give the display a minute or two: it keeps retrying your Wi-Fi until your
  router is back.
- If the display has been off Wi-Fi for 5 minutes, it restarts and tries again by itself.
- Unplug the display, wait 10 seconds, and plug it back in.

## Pictures look wrong, or a picture is skipped

Re-upload the slideshow from the app. A picture that didn't finish uploading is skipped
automatically rather than shown half-finished.

## "Insufficient Disk Space" or "Maximum Storage Space"

A slideshow holds up to **7** pictures. Remove a picture and try again.

## The display shows "SAFE MODE"

Button A was held while it was powered on. Unplug it and plug it back in without holding any button.

## The display is blank, stuck, or keeps restarting

1. Unplug it, wait 10 seconds, and plug it in again.
2. If that doesn't help, reinstall its software with the
   [Pixel Xpress Flasher](repair-with-flasher.md). Your pictures and Wi-Fi settings are kept.
3. Still stuck? Use the Flasher's **erase** option, then set the display up again.

## An update over Wi-Fi failed

See [Updating over Wi-Fi → If an update doesn't work](update-over-wifi.md#if-an-update-doesnt-work).
The display always goes back to working software by itself.
